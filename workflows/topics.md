# Find Topics

If I already gave you a topic, skip this and go to [write.md](write.md).

## Start from my work

When I ask what to post without giving a topic, check my inbox first. In [posts.json](../assets/posts.json), each post's `from` lists the inbox days it used. If there are days in `inbox/` that no post has used yet, follow [Pick topics](#pick-topics) with them.

If the inbox has nothing new, ask me one question: what have I been working on or learning lately? A sentence or two is enough, like something I built, a problem I solved, or a call I made. If I share something, save it to the inbox the way [daily.md](daily.md#save-it) says, then follow [Pick topics](#pick-topics).

If I have nothing, or I ask what's hot, ask me to paste a post I came across and follow [Start from a post](#start-from-a-post). If I don't have one either, suggest topics from my profile and past posts alone. Don't go looking for news on your own.

## Start from a post

Use what I pasted. If I only gave a link and you can't open it, ask me to paste the text. Then follow [Pick topics](#pick-topics), starting with a one-line summary of the post, and make each suggested topic my own take on the same subject. If the post's claims are leaks or come from a single source, mark them as unconfirmed.

## Pick topics

First read [profile.md](../context/profile.md), the day files in `inbox/` that no post has used yet (the last month or so is plenty, plus any older day a waiting idea points to), the idea bank (`ideas` in [posts.json](../assets/posts.json), including ones I asked you to save), and the recent posts in [linkedin_posts.md](../output/linkedin_posts.md). A used day can still hold something its post left out, so it can come back when it fits. Then reply like we're chatting with two or three suggested topics. An idea from the bank can be one of them if it's still among the strongest, and one I asked you to save is a good sign I want to write it. For each, a short paragraph: how I could tell it, why people I want to reach would care, and what from my inbox, work, or views it connects to. For inbox days, say which day.

Use the "Posting angle" in my profile. When a topic would be much stronger with a story from my work, say what kind of story would fit, and I'll tell you if I have one. Avoid repeating recent content. Building on an earlier topic is even better. If [posts.json](../assets/posts.json) has numbers, check which angles and kinds of stories did best for me, and lean toward those, without suggesting the same kind of story every time.

Save each new topic you suggest to the idea bank, as in [maintain.md](maintain.md). Then we talk them through: if I want to write one, go to [write.md](write.md).
