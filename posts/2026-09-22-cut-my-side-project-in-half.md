# I cut my side project in half, and people finally used it

For three months I built a small app for splitting shared expenses with my roommates. It had budgets, charts, reminders, and a dark mode.

Nobody used it, including my roommates.

So I asked them what actually happens. Someone pays, and everyone else forgets to pay them back. That's it.

I removed everything except adding an expense and seeing who owes what. It took one weekend. Two weeks later, all four of us use it.

The features weren't bad. They answered questions nobody was asking yet.
