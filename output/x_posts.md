## 2026-09-08 · Embeddings finally clicked when I searched my own notes

1/ Read about embeddings 3 times. Still didn't get it 😅
What finally worked: searching my own lecture notes with them 🧵

2/ Turned each paragraph of my OS notes into an embedding, then searched "how does the computer juggle several programs at once"

3/ Top result: my paragraph on context switching. It never says "juggle" or "at once" 🤯
It matched on meaning, not words.

4/ That's the whole idea: search by meaning. The vectors are just how the computer tracks what's close to what.

5/ Concept not clicking? Try it on something you know well. Your own notes work great 📝
