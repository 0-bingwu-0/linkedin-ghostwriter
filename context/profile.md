## Background

I'm a third-year computer science student. I learn AI by building small projects with it. Right now I'm building a study helper that answers questions from my lecture slides.

## Posting goals

* **Land an internship:** Let recruiters and engineers see what I build and how I think.
* **Learn in public:** Writing down what I learn helps me understand it, and people correct me when I'm wrong.
* **Meet people:** Connect with other students and engineers building with AI.

## Posting angle

I share what I learn about AI as a student who builds with it:

* What I built, what broke, and how I fixed it.
* New AI models and tools, tried on real student work like studying, coding, and class projects.
* What I wish someone had told me when I started.

Ideas I keep coming back to:

* You learn AI fastest by building something small and real.
* AI makes it easy to get an answer. Understanding why it works is still on you.

## Writing style

* **Language:** English.
* **Natural:** Like telling a friend about something, not a report or an ad. Jargon only when needed, and explained the first time.
* **Sentences:** Mostly short, with natural variety. Don't chase punchlines or impact.
* **Tone:** Warm and sincere. State my view clearly and leave readers room to decide. No unnecessary disclaimers.
* **Specific:** Be accurate. Say what I tried, what happened, and what I took from it. When comparing tools or methods, say where each fits and how they actually differ.
* **Humor:** Light. A bit of self-deprecation is fine. Don't try hard to be funny.
* **Emoji:** None or very few on LinkedIn. Plenty in X Posts, for feeling.
* **Per platform:** A LinkedIn post tells one story or makes one point, plainly and patiently. An X Post is shorter and more relaxed. An X Article is plain and patient, like explaining something to a friend.

## Avoid

* Hype, like calling every release a game changer.
* Sounding like I know more than I do.
