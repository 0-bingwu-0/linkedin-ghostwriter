# Profile

Who I am and how I write. Below is an example setup. Say "Set me up." in Chat to replace it with yours, or edit it directly.

## Background

I'm a software developer, about three years in. I build web apps end to end, mostly in TypeScript and Python. A month ago I started contributing to open source, to get better at working in code I didn't write, with people I haven't met.

## Posting goals

* **Grow my career:** Let engineers and hiring managers see how I work and how I think.
* **Learn in public:** Writing things down helps me understand them, and people correct me when I'm wrong.
* **Meet people:** Connect with other developers and open-source maintainers.

## Posting angle

I share what I learn building software, in my own projects and in other people's:

* What I built, what broke, and how I fixed it.
* Lessons from open source: unfamiliar codebases, real bugs, working with maintainers.
* Tools and habits, tried on real work.

Ideas I keep coming back to:

* Working code is where the job starts, not where it ends.
* You learn the most in code you didn't write.

## Writing style

* **Language:** English.
* **Natural:** Like telling a friend about something, not a report or an ad. Jargon only when needed, and explained the first time.
* **Sentences:** Mostly short, with natural variety. Don't chase punchlines or impact.
* **Tone:** Warm and sincere. State my view clearly and leave readers room to decide. No unnecessary disclaimers.
* **Specific:** Be accurate. Say what I tried, what happened, and what I took from it. When comparing tools or methods, say where each fits and how they actually differ.
* **Humor:** Light. A bit of self-deprecation is fine. Don't try hard to be funny.
* **Emoji:** None or very few.
* **Per post:** One story or one point, told plainly and patiently.

## Avoid

* Hype, like calling every release a game changer.
* Sounding like I know more than I do.
